This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind, and it doesn’t load anything from third parties: fonts, scripts and images are all served from this site. A strict Content Security Policy enforces that in your browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

If you pick a light or dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage, and a flag in session storage makes sure the intro animation plays only once per visit. Neither ever leaves your device.

If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and we delete it when you ask.
