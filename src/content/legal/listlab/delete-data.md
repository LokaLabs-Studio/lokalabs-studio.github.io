## Before you delete: export

Deleting can’t be undone. If you might want your lists later, export them first:

1. Open ListLab and go to **Settings**, then **Your data**.
2. Tap **Export a copy**.
3. Pick a folder for the file.

The copy holds your tasks, lists, routines, alarms, the text of your captures and your settings. Recordings, images, PDFs and AI models stay out of it, so save any you want to keep before you delete.

To move to a new phone, install ListLab there, go to **Settings**, then **Your data**, tap **Restore from a file** and pick the copy.

## Delete everything from the app

1. Open ListLab and go to **Settings**, then **Your data**.
2. Tap **Delete everything**.
3. Confirm with **Delete everything**.

ListLab cancels your alarms and reminders, then erases your tasks, lists, captures, recordings, routines, alarms, suggestions, downloaded AI models and settings from the phone straight away. The next time you open it, it starts like a new install.

Two small things stay, because they’re about the app rather than your data: whether you bought a support purchase, so the icon vault stays open, and the app icon you picked.

## Remove just the AI models

The AI models are the biggest files ListLab keeps, about 3.5&nbsp;GB together. To remove one and keep everything else, go to **Settings**, then **AI**, and tap **Remove** next to the model. Your captures and suggestions stay.

## Or clear the app’s storage in Android

1. Open your phone’s **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **ListLab**.
2. Tap **Storage**, then **Clear storage** (sometimes called Clear data).

This erases everything ListLab stored on the phone, the support record and app icon included.

## Uninstall

Uninstalling ListLab deletes the app and all the data it stored on your phone.

## Copies outside the app

ListLab can’t reach copies stored elsewhere, so check these too:

- **Exported files.** Delete any JSON exports you saved to folders, cloud drives or emails.
- **Your Google backup.** If Android backup is on, a copy of ListLab’s database and settings may be in your Google account. You can manage or delete it in your phone’s settings under Google, then Backup, or at [Google Drive backups](https://drive.google.com/drive/backups).
- **Files you shared in.** Anything you shared into ListLab from another app is still in that app. Deleting it in ListLab doesn’t touch the original.

## Advertising ID

If you watched the optional ad, Google may have used your phone’s Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your phone’s settings under Privacy, then Ads. To manage data Google holds about you, use [Google’s privacy tools](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).

## Purchases

Records of any support purchases are kept by Google Play as part of your order history. You can see them in the Play Store app under your profile, then **Payments and subscriptions**, then **Budget and history**. We can’t delete Google’s records, but we don’t keep a separate list of buyers.
