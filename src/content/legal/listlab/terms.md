## Reminders, alarms and prayer times

ListLab works hard to ring on time. It schedules exact alarms, sets them again after a restart and has a reminder check for the phone settings that get in the way. Still, Android, battery savers, sleeping-app lists, Do Not Disturb rules, a muted phone or a phone that’s switched off can delay or silence a reminder or alarm, and some of that is outside our control.

- **Don’t rely on ListLab alone** for anything where a missed reminder could cause harm, such as medication, medical appointments, caring for someone, or legal and financial deadlines. Keep a second reminder for those.
- **Run the reminder check** after you install ListLab and after big Android updates.
- **Prayer times are calculations.** ListLab works them out on your phone from the method and location you choose. They can differ from your local mosque or authority by a few minutes, so check with them when timing matters.

## Your data and backups

Your data is stored only on your phone (see the [Privacy Policy](/apps/listlab/privacy/)). That means we can’t see it, but it also means we can’t restore it for you. If you uninstall the app, delete its data, lose your phone or reset it without a backup, your lists, notes and recordings may be gone for good.

ListLab gives you an export in Settings, under “Your data”. Keeping regular backups is your responsibility. The export doesn’t include recordings, images or PDFs, so keep anything important from those somewhere else too.
