## Data that stays on your phone

LiftLab is built to work offline, without an account. Everything you enter is saved in a private database (SQLite) inside the app’s own storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it.

That on-device data includes:

- **Your profile:** goal, sex (if you choose to give it), age, height, weight, units, training level, session length, where you train (home or gym), your equipment, the muscle areas you want to focus on, and any injuries or limitations you tell the app about.
- **Your training:** your plan and programs, every workout you log (exercises, sets, weights, reps and effort), the workout in progress, bodyweight entries, badges, streaks and ranks.
- **Your settings:** language, units, rest-timer preferences, the app icon you picked, and whether you’ve supported the app.

Injuries and limitations count as health-related information. LiftLab uses them only on your phone, to avoid exercises that could aggravate them. They are never transmitted.

## Backups you make yourself

- **Export:** in Settings, under “Your data”, you can export everything as a JSON file. Android’s share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes, for example your own cloud drive, an email to yourself or a folder on your phone. Once you send it somewhere, that service’s privacy policy applies to the copy.
- **Restore:** you can restore from a JSON backup in Settings or on the welcome screen. Android’s file picker opens and LiftLab reads only the file you pick. Restoring replaces what’s currently on the phone.

We never receive your exports.

## Android backup

LiftLab allows Android’s built-in backup. If you have backup turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the app’s data to that account and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is a feature of Android and your Google account, not of LiftLab, and Google handles that copy under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can turn backup off, or delete the backed-up copy, in your phone’s settings under Google, then Backup.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

LiftLab has exactly one ad: an optional rewarded video you can watch to open the icon vault (bonus app icons) for 2&nbsp;minutes. There are no banner ads and no pop-up ads.

To make that possible, the app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google:

- **The SDK starts when LiftLab opens,** even if you never watch an ad. It may contact Google at startup.
- **An ad is only requested when you tap “Watch an ad”.** LiftLab asks for non-personalised ads only, which means Google doesn’t use your past activity to pick the ad.
- **What Google may process:** your device’s Advertising ID, your IP address (which gives a rough location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. On newer Android versions, the SDK may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox advertising features.

Google processes this data as an independent company under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can read more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your phone’s settings under Privacy, then Ads (the exact wording varies by phone).

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

LiftLab offers five optional one-time support purchases, from $1.99 to $49.99 (shown in your local currency). Each one opens the icon vault permanently. They are not subscriptions and nothing renews. No purchase is needed to avoid ads, because the only ad is one you choose to watch.

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card or bank details. Google shares standard order records with developers (such as the order number, the product bought, the date and the country), which we may use to answer refund or support questions. The app itself only records, on your phone, which support level you bought.

## Exercise images

LiftLab ships with images for its exercises inside the app. If an image is ever missing from the app, LiftLab downloads it from the public [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) collection hosted on GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`). When that happens, GitHub receives a standard web request that includes your IP address. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Notifications

LiftLab can alert you when a rest period ends, even if your phone is locked. These are local notifications: they are scheduled on your phone by the app itself, with no push service and no server involved. You can turn them off in the app or in Android’s notification settings.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why LiftLab asks for it |
| --- | --- |
| Internet and network state | The optional rewarded ad, Google Play purchases, and the rare missing exercise image. |
| Notifications | Rest-timer alerts. |
| Alarms and reminders | So rest alerts arrive on time. Optional. |
| Vibration, wake lock | Vibration feedback and reliable timers during a workout. |
| Run at startup | Restoring scheduled rest alerts after your phone restarts. |
| Google Play Billing | Optional support purchases. |
| Advertising ID and ad services | Used by Google’s ad SDK for the optional rewarded ad. |

LiftLab doesn’t ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos, and it doesn’t connect to Health Connect or other fitness services. Some Android libraries included in the app declare extra technical permissions (for example, showing a badge on your launcher icon). LiftLab’s own code doesn’t use them to collect anything.

## What we don’t do

- We don’t run accounts, servers or a database of users.
- We don’t include analytics or crash-reporting tools.
- We don’t sell or share your personal information.
- We don’t see your workouts, your profile or your health information.

Google Play gives every developer aggregated statistics, such as install counts, and crash reports that Android users choose to share. Those come from Google Play, not from LiftLab.

## Children

LiftLab is meant for people aged 13 and over and isn’t directed at children. The app won’t accept an age under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can wipe it in Settings, under “Your data”, then “Wipe all data”, or by uninstalling the app. The [Delete your data](/apps/liftlab/delete-data/) page walks through every option, including your Google backup and Advertising ID.

Google keeps ad, purchase and backup data according to its own policies. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as we need it to help you, and we delete it sooner if you ask.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Saudi Arabia’s Personal Data Protection Law, the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or California law), you have rights to access, correct, delete and move your personal data, and to object to some uses of it.

Because LiftLab keeps your data on your phone, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app: export it, edit it or wipe it. For data held by Google (ads, purchases, backups), use [Google’s privacy tools](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy). For anything else, email us and we’ll help. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. In Saudi Arabia, that is the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

## Security

Your LiftLab data sits in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox and your phone’s lock screen and encryption. Connections to Google Play, AdMob and GitHub use encrypted HTTPS. Keep your exported backup files somewhere safe, since they contain your full training history.
