## Before you delete: export

Deleting can’t be undone. If you might want your history later, export it first:

1. Open LiftLab and go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Your data**, tap **Export everything (JSON)**.
3. Choose where to save the file, for example your cloud drive or an email to yourself.

To move to a new phone, install LiftLab there, then choose **Restore from backup** on the welcome screen (or in Settings, under Your data) and pick that file.

## Delete everything from the app

1. Open LiftLab and go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Your data**, tap **Wipe all data**.
3. Confirm with **Delete**.

Your profile, plan, workouts, bodyweight entries and badges are erased from the phone straight away.

## Or clear the app’s storage in Android

1. Open your phone’s **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **LiftLab**.
2. Tap **Storage**, then **Clear storage** (sometimes called Clear data).

This does the same thing as wiping from inside the app.

## Uninstall

Uninstalling LiftLab deletes the app and all the data it stored on your phone.

## Copies outside the app

LiftLab can’t reach copies stored elsewhere, so check these too:

- **Exported files.** Delete any JSON exports you saved to cloud drives, emails or folders.
- **Your Google backup.** If Android backup is on, a copy of the app’s data may be in your Google account. You can manage or delete it in your phone’s settings under Google, then Backup, or at [Google Drive backups](https://drive.google.com/drive/backups).

## Advertising ID

If you watched the optional ad, Google may have used your phone’s Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your phone’s settings under Privacy, then Ads. To manage data Google holds about you, use [Google’s privacy tools](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).

## Purchases

Records of any support purchases are kept by Google Play as part of your order history. You can see them in the Play Store app under your profile, then **Payments and subscriptions**, then **Budget and history**. We can’t delete Google’s records, but we don’t keep a separate list of buyers.
