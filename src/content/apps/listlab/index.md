ListLab is a to-do list for people who forget things. Say it, share it or type it, and ListLab holds on to it until it matters. Everything stays on your phone.

## Drop anything in

Type “dentist friday 3pm #health” and the date, time and list turn into chips you can undo with a tap. Hold the + key to talk, share a message or screenshot from any app, or pull down the Quick Settings tile for a voice note. ListLab keeps the original of whatever you drop in, so you can always go back to what you actually said.

## Reminders that show up

Reminders fire on the minute and come back after a restart or a time-zone change. The reminder check walks you through the phone settings that silence apps, including Samsung’s sleeping apps. Whatever you didn’t finish yesterday moves to today quietly, without a red overdue count.

## Routines planned backwards

Tell ListLab you need to leave at 6:00 and it works out when to get up from your steps and how long each one takes. Run mode then takes you through them with a countdown, reads each step out loud and tells you if you’re falling behind. If you switch prayer times on, ListLab calculates them offline and a routine can start from one.

## A real alarm clock

The alarm fills the lock screen, rings on silent and in Do Not Disturb, and gets louder until you answer it. Dismiss it and your morning routine starts. The morning recap lists today, what carried over and when you need to leave, and can read itself to you.

## Optional AI that stays on your phone

Turn AI on in Settings and ListLab downloads a language model and a speech model to your phone, about 3.5&nbsp;GB in all. After that it transcribes your voice notes, reads the text in screenshots and suggests tasks and reminders as cards in your Inbox. Nothing becomes a task until you tap **Keep**, and the AI never sends what you capture anywhere. Leave AI off and everything else works the same.
