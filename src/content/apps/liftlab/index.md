LiftLab is a complete strength coach that lives entirely on your phone. Answer eight quick questions and start training today.

## It plans your week around you

LiftLab decides which days you train based on how recovered you actually are. Crush a heavy session and it gives your muscles room to rebuild. Miss a few days and it adapts instead of guilt-tripping you. Mark your days off and the schedule works around your life.

## Built on exercise science

Programs follow research-backed principles: progressive overload with double progression, sensible weekly volume per muscle, and rep ranges and rest times tuned to your goal. The Method screen shows the reasoning, with citations, because a coach you can’t question isn’t much of a coach.

## Listens to your energy

A quick check-in before each workout scales the session to the day you’re actually having. Low battery gets a lighter version with full credit. Running on empty gets a 10-minute minimum, a recovery session or a guilt-free skip with your streak protected.

## Works anywhere

Over 870 exercises with images and step-by-step instructions ship inside the app. Basement gym, airplane mode, dead-zone garage: everything works.

## Honest progress

A muscle-by-muscle recovery map, a volume heatmap, personal records, e1RM estimates, warm-up ramps and a plate calculator. Streaks count training days only, so a rest day can never break one.
