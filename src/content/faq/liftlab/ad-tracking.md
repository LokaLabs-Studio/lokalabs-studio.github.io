---
question: Does the ad track me?
group: liftlab
order: 3
---

LiftLab only requests non-personalised ads, so Google doesn’t pick the ad based on your past activity. To show and measure the ad, limit repeats and prevent fraud, Google’s ad SDK can use your device’s Advertising ID and IP address, and it starts when the app opens. Your workouts and profile are never shared with it. The [privacy policy](/apps/liftlab/privacy/#advertising-google-admob) has the details, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone’s settings under Privacy, then Ads.
