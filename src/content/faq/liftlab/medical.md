---
question: Is LiftLab medical advice?
group: liftlab
order: 6
---

No. LiftLab builds plans from the answers you give it, using general training principles. It isn’t a doctor or a physiotherapist. Check with a professional before starting something new, especially with an injury or a health condition, and stop if anything hurts. The [terms](/apps/liftlab/terms/#health-and-safety) say more.
