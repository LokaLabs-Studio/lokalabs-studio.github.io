---
question: How do I move LiftLab to a new phone?
group: liftlab
order: 1
---

On your old phone, open **Settings**, then **Your data**, then **Export everything (JSON)**, and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, install LiftLab and tap **Restore from backup** on the welcome screen.

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may also restore LiftLab’s data automatically when you set up the new phone.
