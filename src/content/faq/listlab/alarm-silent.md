---
question: Will the alarm ring if my phone is on silent?
group: listlab
order: 5
---

Yes. ListLab alarms ring on silent and in Do Not Disturb, like your phone’s own clock app, and fill the lock screen until you snooze or dismiss them. Android asks you to allow **Full-screen alarms** once. If you skipped that, the reminder check shows a button to turn it on.

Ordinary reminders follow your phone’s sound settings. For one you can’t miss, turn on **Must-not-miss reminders** and it repeats until you open it.
