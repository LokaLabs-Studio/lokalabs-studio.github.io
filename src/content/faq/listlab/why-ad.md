question: Why is there an ad in ListLab?
group: listlab
order: 6
---

There’s exactly one, and it only plays if you ask for it. In the icon vault, you can watch a short ad to try the extra app icons for 2&nbsp;minutes. Any icon you apply stays after the vault closes. There are no banners, no pop-ups and nothing to dismiss before you get to your list.
