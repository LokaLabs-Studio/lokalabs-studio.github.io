question: My reminders arrive late or not at all
group: listlab
order: 4
---

Android pauses apps to save battery, and some phones do it hard. Open **Settings**, then **Reminders** and run the **Reminder check**. It lists anything that can hold a reminder back, like exact alarms, notifications or battery limits, with a button to fix each one.

On Samsung phones, also add ListLab to **Never auto sleeping apps** under **Battery**, then **Background usage limits**. The check walks you through it.
