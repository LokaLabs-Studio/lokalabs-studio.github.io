question: Which phones can run the AI?
group: listlab
order: 3
---

You need about 4&nbsp;GB of memory, and 6&nbsp;GB or more makes it comfortable. The models take about 3.5&nbsp;GB of storage, and **Settings**, then **AI** tells you if your phone fits before anything downloads. You can limit downloads to Wi-Fi, or load your own GGUF model under **Bring your own model**.

Without AI, ListLab still does everything else. Voice notes and shared items are saved in Captures for you to play back or read, and quick add still understands dates, lists and repeats.
