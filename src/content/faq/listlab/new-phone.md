---
question: How do I move ListLab to a new phone?
group: listlab
order: 1
---

On your old phone, open **Settings**, then **Your data**, then **Export a copy**, and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, install ListLab, go to **Settings**, then **Your data**, tap **Restore from a file** and pick it.

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may also restore your lists, routines and settings when you set up the new phone. Recordings and AI models don’t travel either way, so download the models again in **Settings**, then **AI**.
