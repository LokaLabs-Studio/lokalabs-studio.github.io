---
question: Does the AI send my notes anywhere?
group: listlab
order: 2
---

No. The models run on your phone, so your voice notes, screenshots and text never leave it. The only network traffic is the one-time model download from our page on GitHub, which sees your IP address like any website would. The [privacy policy](/apps/listlab/privacy/#optional-ai-on-your-phone) has the details.
